What we do

Two services, one desk

Claims recovery

Every pending, queried, deducted or rejected scheme bill, followed up until it is resolved.

  • Complete reconciliation: bills submitted vs. payments received
  • Query replies and re-submissions with the right documents, on time
  • Representations against incorrect deductions, citing the applicable rules
  • Appeals filed within deadlines; escalation of long-pending dues
  • Consolidated dues file, ready for management and auditors

Compliance desk

One compliance calendar for your facility, with supporting records kept inspection-ready.

  • Clinical Establishments registration, fire NOC, pollution board consents and renewals
  • Biomedical waste authorisation, annual returns and records
  • PCPNDT registration, Form F records and reporting
  • Patient-data handling aligned with the DPDP Act, 2023
  • Complaint, incident and staff records maintained and organised

When something goes wrong, your records are ready

A negligence notice, a consumer complaint, a licence query, a staff dispute or long-unpaid dues: in each case, outcomes depend on documentation. We compile the complete, organised record (case files, consents, registers, correspondence and timelines) so your management and professional advisers have everything they need from day one.

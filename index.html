How it works

We work alongside your existing Ayushman desk. Your team keeps handling patients; we handle what happens after discharge.

1. Audit

We go through your claims and list every one that is pending, rejected or paid short, with the amount and the reason.

2. Appeal

For rejected claims, we find what was missing (vitals charts, OT notes, reports) and file the appeal before the deadline.

3. Track

Every month we follow up on every claim and query, and send you a clear report: what is stuck, what got approved, what is next.

4. Train

Every quarter we show your staff which documents cause rejections, so fewer claims get stuck in the first place.

What we promise, and what we don't

We promise a defined job done on time: every claim audited, every appeal filed before its deadline, every month tracked and reported.

We can't guarantee payment. When money is released is decided by the State Health Agency and its budget.

Your data stays protected

We work only under a signed data processing agreement and your written authorisation. Patient information is used only to process your claims, kept access-controlled, and deleted on schedule.

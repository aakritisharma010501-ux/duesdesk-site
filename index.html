Questions NRIs ask

General information only — every case is different, which is why the first assessment is free.

Do I need to travel to India?

For most cases, no. Documents that need your signature can usually be signed and attested at the Indian consulate or embassy where you live, or handled through a properly drafted power of attorney.

Can an NRI inherit and sell property in India?

Generally yes — NRIs can inherit residential and commercial property and sell it, subject to FEMA rules. Agricultural land has extra restrictions. We check your specific case in the free assessment.

How much money can I send abroad from my NRO account?

Up to USD 1 million per financial year from NRO balances, once taxes are paid, with Form 15CA and a CA certificate in Form 15CB.

How do I know if my parents' shares went to IEPF?

Shares move to the government's IEPF fund when dividends go unclaimed for seven consecutive years. A search by name and folio usually tells us within a day or two.
